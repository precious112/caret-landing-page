^ The idea

For decades, design and development lived in separate worlds — you mock a screen in a design tool, someone rebuilds it by hand in code, and the two drift apart from the moment they're created.

Caret is built on a bet that this divide no longer needs to exist. AI is now powerful enough that the design is the code. One source of truth that's design and code at once — versioned in your repo, synced into the app you ship.

It's for the people building the modern frontend — designers moving into code, and frontend engineers who'd rather design where they already work. As AI erases the line between designing and building, Caret is what's on the other side.

^ How it works

Two layers, one repo.

.caret/

A design layer

Everything you design, standardized to React under .caret/ — pages, flows, shared components, and design tokens. Think Figma frames as code, version-controlled alongside your app.

→
your app

Your application

The app you actually ship — in any framework (React, Vue, Svelte, Angular…). Caret stays unopinionated. You design in the first layer, then sync into this one.

^ Live design canvas

Your whole product, on one canvas.

Every page renders on a zoomable, pannable canvas — the focused page runs as live, interactive React; the rest stay fast as cached thumbnails. Switch viewport presets to check responsiveness, and pan out to see how it all fits together.

responsive canvas

^ Visual editing

Edit the real UI, directly.

Click an element to change its text, color, or image inline — written back to the exact line of source, instantly via hot-reload. For anything structural, choose Edit with AI and Caret applies the precise change.

Edit-with-AI and all source edits are powered by Cline, the open-source coding agent Caret is built on.

inline editing

^ Flows & simulation

See how it all connects.

Define user journeys as flow graphs and overlay them on the canvas. Restructure a flow by dragging an edge, then enter simulation mode to click through your app in a device frame — jumping between page states like a real user.

flows

^ Token-driven design system

A guided wizard captures color, type, spacing, and radius into namespaced tokens under .caret/tokens/ — injected into generation so every page stays consistent.

^ Design → app sync

When the design's ready, Caret produces a reviewable plan covering UI, state, routing, and data changes. You accept; it applies. One-way and reversible.

^ A full coding agent

Built on Cline: bring any model, run commands, edit files with reviewable diffs, drive a browser, use MCP tools — human-in-the-loop.

^ Lives in your repo

The design layer is plain React, version-controlled next to your app. No separate file to keep in sync — design-as-code, in git, where your work already is.

⚠️ The builds aren't code-signed yet — one command opens them

Signing certificates cost $99–$500/yr, which we'll add once Caret gains traction. The apps are safe; your OS just shows a one-time warning. The most reliable fix is a single terminal command:

macOSxattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Caret.app
WindowsUnblock-File "$HOME\Downloads\Caret-Windows-UserSetup-x64.exe"
Linuxchmod +x Caret-Linux-x86_64.AppImage && ./Caret-Linux-x86_64.AppImage
